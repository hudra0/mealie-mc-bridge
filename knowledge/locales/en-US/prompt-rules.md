# English wording (en-US)

Rules for the English text of the recipe, written from the en-US corpus of official Monsieur Cuisine recipes. They add to the language-neutral rules; English phrases in quotes are the wording to use. This pack is not verified on a device.

- Official English recipes use British kitchen words: "blender jug" for the bowl, "measuring beaker", "mixer attachment" for the butterfly whisk, "cooking pot" for the cooking basket, "steamer basket", "courgettes", "aubergines", "coriander", "spring onions".
- Text steps are full sentences in the imperative: "Put 200 g water into the blender jug.", "Season to taste with salt and pepper."
- Count eggs, garlic cloves, herb sprigs and bay leaves without a unit: the amount field holds the count and the unit stays empty, with the item in the name ("garlic cloves", "parsley sprigs", "eggs (medium)").
- Write units as the Units section spells them: g, tsp, tbsp, pinch or pinches, big pinch or big pinches. Never write cups, ounces or pounds; convert them to g.

## Ingredient names

- Write each ingredient list name the way Monsieur Cuisine recipes spell it, so the bridge can link it to the app's ingredient database. Only an exact match apart from case and spacing gets an id; a near miss gets none. Names start in lower case: "onions", "garlic cloves", "carrots", "eggs (medium)". Qualifiers in parentheses: "plain flour (type 405)", "cream (30% fat)", "milk (fresh milk, 3.5% fat)". Form and state after a comma: "yeast, fresh", "pepper, black, ground", "tomatoes, chopped, from a tin/jar". To-taste items as in the amounts rules: "salt to taste".
- Keep the preparation out of the list name ("onions", not "onions, diced"); the step that cuts them says how. Steps then use the name without its qualifiers: "the onions" for "onions, red".
- When the user message lists catalog names for an ingredient line, use the fitting one verbatim. Keep serving and buying notes out of the name unless a catalog name has them: "for garnish", "to serve", "fresh" and "from a tin" go into the step text ("Garnish with parsley."), so the list says "coconut milk", not "coconut milk from a tin".
