# German wording (de-DE)

Rules for the German text of the recipe, derived from the de-DE corpus. They add to the language-neutral rules; German phrases in quotes are the wording to use.

- Official recipes write "dazugeben", not "zugeben".
- Never write "Deckel schließen" (the lid is always closed) or "auf einem Teller abwiegen".
- Keep Stück for eggs, garlic cloves (name "Knoblauchzehen"), herb stems (name "Petersilienstiele") and bay leaves.
- Write units as the Units section spells them: g, Tl, El, Prise or Prisen, Msp. and Stück.
- Text step titles are short imperatives with the verb last: "Zwiebel vorbereiten" over the description "Zwiebel schälen, halbieren und in den Mixbehälter geben.", "Abschmecken" over "Mit Salz und Pfeffer abschmecken.".

## Ingredient names

- Write each ingredient list name the way Monsieur Cuisine recipes spell it, so the bridge can link it to the app's ingredient database. Only an exact match apart from case and spacing gets an id; a near miss gets none. Counted items in the plural: "Zwiebeln", "Knoblauchzehen", "Möhren", "Eier (Größe M)". Qualifiers in parentheses: "Mehl (Type 405)", "Sahne (30 % Fett)", "Joghurt natur (3,5 % Fett)". Form and state after a comma: "Hefe, frisch", "Pfeffer, schwarz, gemahlen". To-taste items as in the amounts rules: "Salz zum Abschmecken".
- Keep the preparation out of the list name ("Zwiebeln", not "Zwiebeln, gewürfelt"); the step that cuts them says how. Steps then use the name without its qualifiers: "die Zwiebeln" for "Zwiebeln (rot)".
- When the user message lists catalog names for an ingredient line, take one that names the same product and write it verbatim. Among those, prefer a name without preparation, serving notes or amounts: "Kirschtomaten", not "Kirschtomaten, halbiert"; "Salz", not "½ Tl Salz". Keep a product qualifier the ingredient needs, such as "frisch", "getrocknet" or "aus der Dose": "Hefe, frisch", "Tomaten, stückig aus der Dose/Glas". Serving notes such as "zum Garnieren" and "zum Servieren" go into the step text ("Mit Petersilie garnieren."). When no listed name is the same product, write your own name by the rules above.
